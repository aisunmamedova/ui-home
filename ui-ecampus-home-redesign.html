Frequently Asked Questions

What is UI eCampus?
UI eCampus is the digital learning platform of the University of Ibadan, designed to make high-quality postgraduate education accessible to professionals, graduates, and lifelong learners through flexible online study.
Why was UI eCampus created?
UI eCampus was established to extend the University of Ibadan’s tradition of academic excellence to a new generation of learners who require flexible and accessible learning opportunities.
Is UI eCampus part of the University of Ibadan?
Yes. UI eCampus operates under the academic authority of the University of Ibadan and delivers programmes that meet the University’s quality standards.
Can I earn a University of Ibadan degree online?
Yes. Eligible students can earn University of Ibadan postgraduate qualifications through UI eCampus.
Who should study at UI eCampus?
UI eCampus is ideal for working professionals, business leaders, entrepreneurs, government officials, researchers, and graduates seeking career advancement.
Is UI eCampus recognised?
Yes. Programmes offered through UI eCampus are delivered under the University of Ibadan, one of Africa’s most respected universities.
Can I study while working full-time?
Yes. The programmes are designed specifically for professionals who need flexibility while pursuing higher education.
Can students outside Nigeria apply?
Yes. Students from anywhere in the world can apply and participate in programmes offered through UI eCampus.
What programmes are available?
UI eCampus offers postgraduate diplomas, master’s degrees, professional master’s programmes, and doctoral programmes across multiple disciplines.
Why should I choose UI eCampus?
UI eCampus combines the prestige of the University of Ibadan with the convenience and flexibility of online learning.
Is online learning as effective as traditional learning?
Yes. Modern online learning combines flexibility, academic support, digital resources, and interactive learning experiences to support student success.
Do I need to take an English proficiency test?
Yes. All postgraduate applicants complete the Test of Proficiency in English (TPE), a University of Ibadan Senate-approved requirement for all postgraduate programmes. The test is conducted fully online, takes approximately one hour, and has a pass mark of 40%. It is completed before allotment to your cohort, and test access details are shared after you submit your application – no campus visit is required.
